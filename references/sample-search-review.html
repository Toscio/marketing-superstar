InnovaTeQ and Felelősségteljes IT: SEO and AI-search review

A prioritised list for the new innovateq.io (still on staging) and the live felelosit.hu. Each item says what to change, why it matters, how to fix it and roughly how long it takes. Everything was checked read-only: no forms submitted, no load tests.

Checked: staging innovateq-website.innovateq-solutions.workers.dev, current innovateq.io (WordPress), felelosit.hu. Data: DataForSEO (Google UK, US and Hungary), Lighthouse 12, live ChatGPT and Perplexity answers.

Summary

  1. The new InnovaTeQ site is technically very good. It is fast (Lighthouse performance 100 on mobile), has clean titles and one H1 per page, and already canonicalises to innovateq.io. Most of the work is at the switch-over, not in the build.
  2. Launch blockers: 18 of the 24 URLs in the current sitemap return 404 on the new site, the new site has no sitemap, and the good llms.txt the old site had is gone. All three are an afternoon's work.
  3. There is little ranking to lose. innovateq.io ranks for almost nothing in Google today (2 keywords, both outside the top 20). The real assets are the brand result and about 20 linking domains, and both go to the homepage.
  4. The bigger problem is name confusion. A Google search for "innovateq" is led by an unrelated Omani company (iNNOVATEQ, innovateq.digital), with an Australian and a UK namesake close behind. Entity signals (schema, profiles, consistent legal name) matter more here than keywords.
  5. AI assistants describe InnovaTeQ as a training company, because that is what the current site says. They will follow the new site, but only once it is crawled and the facts are stated plainly.
  6. felelosit.hu is well built but not yet recognised. Its schema, hreflang, sitemap and llms.txt are all in place. But Google shows the privacy page first for the brand name, Perplexity finds nothing, and ChatGPT invents a description ("an ethics initiative"). It needs indexing and entity work, then a few Hungarian explainer pages where the search demand is.
  7. The two brands do not compete. They serve different languages, audiences and problems. Ádám links them, and the right move is a deliberate link through him (schema and a line on each site), not hreflang.

A. Pre-launch blockers

BLOCKER

Redirect the old URLs

Effort: 1–2 h
What
The current sitemap lists 24 URLs. On staging, 6 exist (home, about, blogs, workshops, legal notice, privacy) and 18 return 404, including the whole /corporate-training/ section, /consulting-services/, /frequently-asked-questions/ and /book-a-call/. The four old blog posts keep the same paths, which is good.
Why
Without 301s, any bookmarks, LinkedIn posts, course links and AI-answer citations that point at these pages become dead ends, and Google drops them without passing their value on. ChatGPT currently cites /corporate-training/ and /workshops/ by URL.
Fix
Add a _redirects file (Workers static assets supports it) with permanent 301s, one line per old URL. Suggested map below. Keep it at least a year.

BLOCKER

Publish a sitemap and point robots.txt at it

Effort: 30 min
What
/sitemap.xml returns 404 on staging, and robots.txt holds only Cloudflare's content-signal comments with no Sitemap: line.
Why
At switch-over, Search Console still holds the old sitemap_index.xml. A fresh sitemap is how Google learns the new pages quickly and drops the old ones.
Fix
Add @astrojs/sitemap with site: "https://innovateq.io" (it writes /sitemap-index.xml), add Sitemap: https://innovateq.io/sitemap-index.xml to robots.txt, and after launch submit it in Search Console and remove the old one.
BLOCKER

Keep the staging host out of the index

Effort: 15 min
What
The workers.dev host is fully open: no noindex tag, no X-Robots-Tag header, and robots.txt allows everything. The canonicals already point to innovateq.io, which helps a lot.
Why
A canonical is a hint, not a rule. If anyone links to the workers.dev URL, Google may index it as a duplicate, and AI crawlers ignore canonicals entirely.
Fix
Send X-Robots-Tag: noindex only when the request host ends in workers.dev (a few lines in the Worker, or a Cloudflare Transform Rule). After launch, either keep that rule or turn off the workers.dev route in the Worker settings. Do not put noindex in the HTML itself: it would ship to production.
BLOCKER

Bring back llms.txt

Effort: 30 min
What
The live site serves an /llms.txt (from Rank Math). The new site returns 404 for it.
Why
It is a cheap, readable summary for AI crawlers and assistants. Removing it at the exact moment the positioning changes leaves them with less to go on.
Fix
Write it by hand in public/llms.txt: one paragraph on who InnovaTeQ is (independent performance and observability advisory, Budapest, founded by Ádám Tóth, legal entity, what it does not do), then links to Services, Problems, Workshops, About and each article with a one-line summary. A starter is in the appendix.
LAUNCH DAY

Switch-over checklist

Effort: 1 h on the day
What
The DNS move and the steps that follow it.
Why
Google notices changes faster when told, and the old WordPress install keeps publishing /xmlrpc.php, /wp-json/ and feeds if it is left running on a subdomain.
Fix
Keep www → apex as a 301 (it is today). Confirm the Search Console property is a Domain property so it survives the change. Submit the new sitemap, then use URL Inspection on the home, services and problems pages. Crawl the old sitemap list once to confirm every line gets a 301 or a 200. Keep a backup of the WordPress database, then retire it rather than leaving it live somewhere.
IMPORTANT

Use one legal name everywhere

Effort: 15 min
What
The new site says "InnovaTeQ Solutions Ltd." in the schema and legal notice (with a double full stop: "Ltd.."), while Adam's one-pager and the Hungarian registers use "InnovaTeQ Solutions Kft.". The brand is also spelled "InnovateQ" on the current site title and "InnovaTeQ" on the new one.
Why
Search engines and AI models match a business across the web by its name, registration number and address. Mixed names weaken the match, and here there is a real UK company called INNOVATEQ LIMITED that "Ltd." pulls towards.
Fix
Use "InnovaTeQ Solutions Kft." as legalName (add "Ltd." as an English gloss only if Adam wants it), fix the double stop, and use the spelling "InnovaTeQ" on every profile.
IMPORTANT

The contact form posts to mailto:

Effort: 1 h
What
The contact form's action is mailto:info@innovateq.io. (Not tested, as agreed; this comes from reading the HTML.)
Why
This is a conversion issue more than an SEO one, but it is the page all the SEO work sends people to. On many phones and on work machines without a mail client, a mailto form does nothing or opens an empty email.
Fix
Send it to HubSpot like the newsletter form already does (the endpoint is already allowed in the CSP), and show the email address as plain text as a fallback.

B. Technical

IN GOOD SHAPE

Titles, descriptions, headings, speed, mobile

No action
What
Every page has a unique title and meta description, exactly one H1, and a self-canonical. Lighthouse scores are 100 for performance, SEO and best practice on mobile and desktop. Largest paint is 1.4 s on mobile with no layout shift, against 2.5 s on the current WordPress site. HSTS, CSP and the other security headers are all set.
Why
This is better than most consultancy sites and leaves nothing to fix on Core Web Vitals. (Field data from real Chrome users does not exist yet for a site this small, so these are lab figures.)
Fix
One small thing: Lighthouse flags low colour contrast on some text (accessibility 96). Darken the light grey text a shade.
IMPORTANT

Richer structured data

Effort: 2–3 h
What
Every page carries the same two schema items: Organization (name, legal name, email, logo) and WebSite. There is no address, no sameAs links to profiles, no founder, no Service items and no article markup on the blog posts.
Why
With a crowded name, schema is how Google and AI systems tell this InnovaTeQ apart from the Omani, Australian and UK ones. Article markup with a date and author also helps the posts get cited.
Fix
Extend the Organization to ProfessionalService with address (Budapest), vatID, taxID, foundingDate, areaServed, knowsAbout (performance engineering, observability, incident analysis) and sameAs (LinkedIn company page, Facebook innovateqio, YouTube academy channel, academy.innovateq.io, europeantechmap.eu listing). Add a Person for Ádám Tóth (founder, sameAs his LinkedIn). Add BlogPosting with datePublished, dateModified and author on each post, and BreadcrumbList on inner pages. A sample is in the appendix.
IMPORTANT

Dates and an author on articles

Effort: 1 h
What
The five posts show no publish date and no named author.
Why
Google and AI engines lean on visible dates and authors to judge whether a technical article is current and written by someone who knows the subject. Ádám's name on the post is the strongest trust signal the site has.
Fix
Show "Ádám Tóth · 12 March 2026" (or similar) under each post title, with a one-line bio and link to the About page at the end.

A proper social preview image

Effort: 30 min
What
og:image is the 270×197 logo on every page, and the Twitter card is summary.
Why
When Adam or anyone shares a link on LinkedIn, the preview is a small, cropped logo. A 1200×630 image gets a full-width card.
Fix
Make one 1200×630 image (logo plus the headline "Clarity for critical digital services"), use it as the default, and switch to summary_large_image.
NICE TO HAVE

Internal links and images

Effort: 1 h
What
Navigation is clean and every page is reachable from the menu. The home page deep-links into each problem and service. The only images are the logo, and its alt text is fine. Blog posts do not link to the service that fits them.
Why
Links from articles to services tell Google which page answers the commercial question, and move readers towards contact.
Fix
End each post with one sentence and a link to the matching problem or service (for example "When scaling is not the solution" → /problems/#slow-unstable). Link the services page back to the two or three most relevant posts.

C. Content and keyword fit

CONTEXT

What the old domain ranks for, and what could be lost

—
What
DataForSEO finds innovateq.io ranking for 2 Google keywords in the US ("innoq" at position 49, and "what is learnworlds" at 25 via the academy subdomain) and none in the UK or Hungary. Backlinks: 44 from about 20 domains. Nearly all point to the homepage, and most are low-quality directory or spam sites. The only relevant ones are theorg.com, testingsaas.nl and kernelpanicpodcast.com, plus links into the academy.
Why
The launch carries very little ranking risk. What matters is that the homepage keeps its URL (it does), that the brand search result updates to the new description, and that the redirects in section A are in place.
Fix
Nothing beyond section A. Do not disavow the spam links; Google ignores them.
IMPORTANT

The name is shared, so win the brand search on purpose

Effort: 2–4 h, then ongoing
What
Google UK for "innovateq": 1 innovateq.digital (Oman), 2 its LinkedIn, 3 innovateq.com.au, 4 academy.innovateq.io, 5 the InnovaTeQ Facebook page (Budapest), 6 a UK Companies House record. innovateq.io itself is not on page one for its own name. For "innovateq solutions" it is second. Google Hungary is similar and also shows the YouTube academy channel.
Why
Prospects who hear the name from Adam will search it. They need to land on innovateq.io, not on an oil-and-gas software firm in Oman.
Fix
Put "InnovaTeQ, Budapest" and "performance and observability" together in the home title or first paragraph. Create or complete a LinkedIn company page and a Google Business Profile under the exact name, both linking to innovateq.io. Use the same logo and description on Facebook, YouTube, the academy and europeantechmap.eu. Ask the handful of real partners (testingsaas.nl, theorg.com, podcast pages) to update their link text to "InnovaTeQ, performance and observability advisory".
IMPORTANT

Search demand is tiny, so write for problems, not categories

Effort: ongoing, about 1 article a month
What
Monthly UK searches: "performance testing training" 20, "incident root cause analysis" 20, "observability consulting" 10, "performance testing consultancy" 10, "monitoring strategy" 10, "performance engineering consulting" too few to report. Category keywords will not bring buyers.
Why
Buyers for this kind of advisory work search for their symptom ("why is our app slow after scaling", "incidents keep recurring") or ask an AI assistant. They rarely search for "observability consultancy".
Fix
Keep writing posts named after the situations on the Problems page. "When scaling is not the solution" is exactly right. Each post should open with a two- or three-sentence direct answer, include one concrete example or number from real work (anonymised), and link to the matching service. Measure success by brand searches and enquiries, not keyword rank.
DECISION

Say where training went

Effort: 30 min
What
The old site leads with "Concept-driven IT training". The new one says "It is not for organisations looking for a training catalogue", while the academy still sells courses.
Why
Anyone arriving from an old training link, or an AI answer that still says "training company", should see where training now lives without being turned away.
Fix
One sentence on the Workshops page: "Looking for self-paced courses? They are at academy.innovateq.io." The redirects then send the old course URLs somewhere useful.

D. GEO: how AI search sees InnovaTeQ

CONTEXT

What the engines say today

—
What
ChatGPT (with web search): "a corporate IT training and capability development company specializing in performance engineering, observability, testing, and operations", citing /about/, /corporate-training/ and /workshops/.
Perplexity: "an IT education and advisory company … role-based corporate training, hands-on workshops, online courses, and consulting", "available in English and Hungarian".
Perplexity, asked for independent performance and observability consultants in Hungary or Europe, lists "Ádám Tóth / InnovaTeQ (Hungary)" first. That is a good sign.
Google AI Overview appears on the "innovateq" results page, where the shared name makes it unreliable.
Why
The engines are accurate about the old site, so they will describe the new positioning once they re-crawl it. The redirects and llms.txt decide how fast that happens and which pages they cite.
Fix
Re-run these four questions two to four weeks after launch. If ChatGPT still says "training company", check that the old /corporate-training/ URLs redirect and that the About page states the new focus in its first sentence.
IMPORTANT

Make the entity unmistakable in the first lines

Effort: 1 h
What
The home and About pages describe what InnovaTeQ does well but never say where it is, who founded it, when or for whom in one quotable sentence.
Why
AI answers are assembled from short passages. A self-contained sentence with name, place, founder and focus is what gets lifted and cited, and it separates InnovaTeQ from its namesakes.
Fix
Open the About page with a sentence like: "InnovaTeQ Solutions Kft. is an independent performance and observability advisory practice in Budapest, founded by Ádám Tóth in [year], working with [sectors] on critical digital services in English and Hungarian."
IMPORTANT

Add facts worth citing, and a short FAQ

Effort: 2–3 h
What
The site has strong principles but few checkable facts: no years of experience, sectors, number of engagements, certifications (for example Dynatrace), speaking or published work. The old FAQ page is gone.
Why
Assistants prefer to cite pages that contain specific, verifiable statements. A question-and-answer block also matches how people phrase questions to them.
Fix
Add a "Facts" line to About (years in performance engineering, sectors, languages, certifications, talks). Add five or six FAQs to Services, for example "How is this different from buying a monitoring tool?", "How long does an investigation take?", "Do you work remotely outside Hungary?", "Do you implement the fixes?", marked up as FAQPage. Google no longer shows FAQ rich results for most sites, but AI engines read the markup and the text.
CHECK

Cloudflare's AI settings

Effort: 10 min
What
robots.txt is Cloudflare's managed content-signals file, with no rules below the comments. Cloudflare can also block AI crawlers at the edge, separately from robots.txt.
Why
If "Block AI bots" is on in the Cloudflare dashboard, ChatGPT and Perplexity cannot read the new site at all, whatever robots.txt says.
Fix
In Cloudflare, under Security → Bots, leave AI crawlers allowed. If you want to keep training opt-outs, add Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no under a User-agent: * line, which allows AI answers while declining training.

A defining sentence at the top of the homepage

Effort: 15 min
What
The H1 ("Informatikai szakértő a teljesítés és az átadás-átvétel tisztázásához") says what it does, but the brand name and the words "független szakértői vélemény" do not appear together in one sentence near the top.
Why
It is the sentence AI engines will quote. Right now they fill the gap by guessing from the name.
Fix
Add one line under the H1, for example: "A Felelősségteljes IT (Practical Solutions Kft.) független informatikai szakértői támogatást ad IT-teljesítési vitákhoz, szoftver átadás-átvételhez és SLA-kérdésekhez Magyarországon."
IMPORTANT

Say clearly that this is not court-appointed expert work

Effort: 30 min
What
The Google.hu results for "informatikai szakértő teljesítési vita" are all court-appointed experts (igazságügyi informatikai szakértő) and academic papers on them. The site's closest target term, "informatikai szakértő", has only about 10 searches a month.
Why
Searchers and AI engines will compare felelosit.hu with court experts. The site already says it is not legal representation, but it does not say how it relates to a court-appointed expert, which is the first question a lawyer will ask.
Fix
Add an FAQ: "Igazságügyi szakértő helyett?" (Instead of a court expert?), answered plainly: a private expert opinion (magánszakértői vélemény) before or alongside litigation, and when a court expert is still needed. This also targets the phrase "magánszakértő".
IMPORTANT

Write for the Hungarian searches that exist

Effort: 3–4 h per article
What
Monthly Google searches in Hungary: "sla" 1,300, "teljesítésigazolás" 480, "sla jelentése" 390, "it audit" 30, "it tanácsadás" 30, "szoftverfejlesztési szerződés" 20, "informatikai tanácsadás" 20 (a high advertising bid, €9.51 a click, so buyers use it). There is no blog or article section.
Why
"Teljesítésigazolás" and "SLA" are the site's exact subject, and people search for them to understand a term, often just before a dispute. A clear explainer is the easiest route to rankings and AI citations.
Fix
Start a small Tudástár (knowledge base) with three pages: "Mi az SLA, és mit jelent a gyakorlatban?", "Teljesítésigazolás IT-projektben: mikor adható ki, és mikor ne?" and "Szoftver átadás-átvétel: mit kell dokumentálni?". Each opens with a two-sentence answer, includes a checklist, and links to the matching service.

How the two sites relate

NO CONFLICT

Overlap and cannibalisation

—
What
InnovaTeQ is English, aimed at engineering and technology leaders, about why services slow down or fail. Felelősségteljes IT is Hungarian, aimed at management and lawyers, about proving whether IT was delivered as contracted. They share a founder, Ádám, and neighbouring themes (SLAs, monitoring evidence), but not keywords, pages or language.
Why
They cannot compete in search, and hreflang does not apply. Hreflang is only for the same content in different languages, and linking these two with it would confuse Google.
Fix
No hreflang between them. Keep felelosit.hu's own hu-HU and x-default tags as they are.
IMPORTANT

Connect them through Ádám

Effort: 1 h
What
Neither site links to the other. felelosit.hu mentions InnovaTeQ only inside Ádám's LinkedIn URL. InnovaTeQ's Services page has a "representation" section and says it does not provide legal representation, which is where felelosit.hu fits.
Why
Each brand makes the other more credible: InnovaTeQ shows the technical depth behind Felelősségteljes IT's expert opinions, and Felelősségteljes IT shows InnovaTeQ has a contract and legal partner in Hungary. A linked person entity is also how AI engines learn that the same expert runs both.
Fix
Give Ádám one Person @id (for example https://innovateq.io/#adam-toth) and reference it from felelosit.hu's schema, with worksFor both organisations and sameAs his LinkedIn. Add one line to Ádám's bio on each site ("also founder of InnovaTeQ, performance and observability advisory" / "társalapító: Felelősségteljes IT, IT-szerződéses és teljesítési szakértés"). On InnovaTeQ's Services page, under representation, add: "For Hungarian contract and SLA disputes, see our partner practice Felelősségteljes IT."
